# Google Developer Style Guide — Word List Reference

Preferred usage for commonly encountered terms. For the full word list see: https://developers.google.com/style/word-list

---

## A

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **above / below** | Avoid for cross-references — use "the following" or link directly instead. |
| **allow / allowlist** | Preferred over "whitelist." |
| **app** | Use for end-user programs (mobile/web). "Application" OK for enterprise context. |
| **API** | Don't use to describe a single method or class; reserve for complete interfaces. |

## B

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **backend** | One word, no hyphen. |
| **blacklist** | Avoid. Use "denylist," "blocklist," or "excludelist." |

## C

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **can** | Use for ability or permission (not "may," which is for legal/policy contexts). |
| **codebase** | One word, no hyphen. |
| **console** | Never use standalone. Always specify: "Google Cloud console," "Admin console." |
| **currently** | Avoid in permanent documentation — it becomes stale. |

## D

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **data** | Treat as singular: "the data is," not "the data are." |
| **denylist** | Preferred over "blacklist." |
| **DevOps** | Capitalized as shown. No expansion needed. |
| **disable** | Prefer precise alternatives: "turn off," "unavailable," "inactive." |

## E

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **enable** | Prefer precise alternatives: "turn on," "available," "active." |
| **eventually** | Avoid in permanent docs — too vague and becomes stale. |

## F

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **frontend** | One word, no hyphen. |

## H

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **health check** | Two words when used as a noun. For computer systems only — avoid medical connotations. Prefer "responsive" over "healthy." |

## I

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **indexes** | Preferred plural of "index" (not "indices") unless domain-specific. |

## L

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **latest** | Avoid in permanent documentation — becomes stale. |
| **login** | Noun/adjective: "the login page." Verb: "log in" (two words). |
| **log in / log out** | Two words as verbs. "Sign in / sign out" generally preferred. |

## M

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **master** | Never use paired with "slave." Use "primary," "main," or "controller." |
| **may** | Reserve for legal/policy situations. Use "can" for ability or permission. |

## N

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **new** | Avoid in permanent docs — becomes stale quickly. |
| **NoSQL** | Capitalized as shown. |
| **now** | Avoid in permanent docs — becomes stale. |

## O

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **OAuth 2.0** | Capitalized and versioned as shown. |

## P

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **parent / child** | Preferred over "master / slave" for node relationships. |
| **please** | Minimize in instructions. Use only when asking for inconvenient actions. |

## S

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **should** | Indicates a recommendation, not a requirement (use "must" for requirements). |
| **sign in / sign out** | Generally preferred over "log in / log out." |
| **slave** | Avoid. Use "replica," "secondary," "worker," or "child." |
| **simply / just / easy** | Remove from procedures — they're condescending. |
| **soon** | Avoid in permanent docs — becomes stale. |

## W

| Term | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **whitelist** | Avoid. Use "allowlist." |

## Hyphens Quick Reference

| Term | Form |
|------|------|
| backend | one word |
| codebase | one word |
| frontend | one word |
| high-availability | hyphenated as adjective |
| load-balancing | hyphenated as adjective |
| real-time | hyphenated as adjective, "in real time" as adverb |
| set up | two words as verb; "setup" as noun/adjective |

---

## Stale Language to Avoid in Permanent Docs

These words make documentation go out of date:
- currently, now, at this time
- soon, eventually, in the future
- latest, newest, new
- recently, just released
